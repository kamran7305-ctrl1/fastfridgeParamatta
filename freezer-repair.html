Common Problems

Common Freezer Problems We Repair

If your freezer is doing one of these, a technician can usually find the cause quickly.

Freezer not freezing

Food is soft, ice cream is runny or the freezer only feels cool. A sign the system is not making enough cold.

Heavy frost or ice build-up

Thick frost on the back wall or around the door. Usually a defrost fault or a seal that is not closing.

Freezer cold, fridge warm

Cold air is not reaching the fridge section. Think iced evaporator, fan or damper.

Freezer not running at all

Silent and dark. Could be power, the controller, a start relay or the compressor.

Loud buzzing or clicking

A failing fan motor, a tired relay or ice touching the fan blade.

Water leaking

Puddles under the freezer or water inside are most often a frozen or blocked defrost drain.

If the freezer is cold but your fridge is warm, see our fridge not cooling repair page.

Freezer Types

Chest, Upright and Fridge-Freezer Repair

Each type fails in its own way. Knowing yours helps us bring the right parts.

Chest freezers

Simple designs, so faults are usually lid seals, thermostats, condenser dirt or the compressor. Ice around the lid is a warning sign.

Upright freezers

Frost-free models rely on fans, defrost heaters and sensors. Manual defrost models fail more on seals and thermostats.

Fridge-freezers

Top mount, bottom mount, French door and side-by-side. The freezer and fridge often share one evaporator, so a freezer fault can warm the fridge too.

Frost-Free or Manual Defrost

Frost-Free vs Manual Defrost Freezers

How your freezer defrosts changes what usually goes wrong.

Frost-freeManual defrost
How it defrosts Automatic, with a heater, timer and sensor You switch it off and defrost by hand
Frost build-up Should stay light Builds up slowly and needs regular defrosting
Usual faults Defrost heater, timer, sensor, fan Seals, thermostat, compressor
Typical type Most upright freezers and fridge-freezers Many chest freezers and older models

Heavy frost on a frost-free freezer is a fault, not normal. On a manual defrost freezer, a thin layer is expected but thick ice means the seal or thermostat needs a look.

When It Stops

Freezer Stopped? What to Do With Your Food

Frozen food holds its cold for a while if you do the right things.

Do this

  • Keep the door or lid shut. A full, closed freezer stays cold far longer than one opened repeatedly.
  • Check the power point, plug and switch.
  • If a neighbour or friend has freezer space, move the most valuable food first.
  • Check the food: ice crystals and hard food are a good sign.
  • Note the brand, model and any error code before you call.

Avoid this

  • Do not chip ice off the back wall with a knife or screwdriver. A punctured coil is a costly repair.
  • Do not refreeze food that has thawed and sat above 5°C for more than 4 hours.
  • Do not use a hairdryer near the electrics.
  • If you smell burning or hear sparking, switch it off at the wall and call us.

Honest Advice

Is It Worth Repairing a Freezer?

Usually, yes, with a few exceptions.

Usually worth repairing

The freezer is under about 10 years old and the repair costs less than half the price of a similar new freezer. A new fan, heater, sensor or seal is almost always worth it.

Usually not worth it

A leaking sealed system on a 15-year-old freezer, or a repair that costs more than half the price of a new one. We will tell you before you spend anything.
