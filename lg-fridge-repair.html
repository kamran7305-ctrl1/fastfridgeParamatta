What we fix

Common LG Fridge Faults in Parramatta

These are the problems we are called to most often on LG fridges and freezers. If yours is different, book anyway and we will find it.

  • Not cooling

    The compressor runs but the fridge stays warm. Often a dirty condenser, a failed fan or a sealed-system fault. Read our fridge not cooling repair page for more.

  • Warm fridge, cold freezer

    Cold air is not reaching the fresh-food side. Frosted evaporator, fan, damper or defrost parts.

  • Display codes

    Er FF, Er rF, Er dH, Er CF and others. The code tells us which system to test.

  • Frost and ice build-up

    Frost on the back wall or ice around a fan, usually a defrost heater, sensor or drain.

  • Ice maker or dispenser trouble

    No ice, small ice or a frozen chute. We test the water line, inlet valve, ice maker fan and sensor.

  • Water leaks

    Puddles under the fridge or in the crisper are normally a frozen or blocked defrost drain.

  • Buzzing, clicking and loud running

    Fan motors, a tired relay or a struggling compressor. Worth fixing before it fails.

  • Fridge dead or tripping power

    A fridge that will not start, or that trips the switch, can have a board, relay or heater fault. Unplug it and call us.

Error codes

LG Fridge Error Codes and What to Try

These are the codes we hear about most, based on LG's own support information. LG can change what a code means between model years, so check your manual too.

CodeUsually meansWhat you can try
Er FFFreezer fan problemOften frost around the fan. Manual defrost: unplug, empty and leave the doors open for about 8 hours. If it returns, book a repair.
Er rFFridge fan problemSame cause and the same defrost steps as Er FF. If it stays, book a repair.

Before you reset anything, note the code. Repeated resets can hide a fault from the technician, and a fan or defrost code that keeps coming back needs a repair.

Linear compressor

LG Linear Compressor and Inverter Faults

Many LG fridges use an inverter linear compressor, and LG has offered long compressor warranties on many models. Check your purchase date, the model number and LG's current terms before paying for a repair. Cover may mean a free fix.

Once cover has ended, we test the compressor, the inverter board, the relay and the fans first. A failed board or relay is often mistaken for a dead compressor and costs far less. If the sealed system is leaking, the leak is fixed first and the gas is recharged. Our compressor repair and fridge regas pages cover both.

Why it happens

LG Fridge Not Cooling? Start Here

An LG fridge makes its cold in the freezer and a fan moves it across to the fridge, which is why the freezer can be fine while the fridge is warm. When frost builds on the evaporator it chokes that airflow. The usual causes are a defrost heater, a defrost sensor or a blocked drain.

Try three quick checks first: close the doors firmly, pull the fridge a few centimetres off the wall and keep the vents clear of food. If it is still warm, book a repair. For a longer list of causes, see why a fridge stops cooling.

Is the food at risk?

Food above 5°C for over 4 hours should be thrown out. Keep the doors closed and move meat and dairy into a cooler with ice. Need us out of hours? See emergency fridge repair.

Honest advice

Is It Worth Repairing an LG Fridge?

Usually yes. Repair when the fix costs less than half the price of a similar new fridge and the fridge is under about 10 years old. A new fan, heater, sensor or seal on a healthy fridge nearly always pays off. A leaking sealed system on a 15-year-old fridge often does not, and we tell you before you spend a cent.

LG Fridge Repair Parramatta FAQs

Where can I get LG fridge repair in Parramatta?

Right here. Our licensed technicians are based locally and repair LG fridges across Parramatta and the suburbs nearby. We are an independent repairer and not an LG service centre. Book online and we confirm a time, usually the same day when you book early.

How much does LG fridge repair cost in Parramatta?

Most LG repairs cost $150 to $450 with parts included, and the call-out is usually $60 to $200. A control board costs $250 to $600 and a compressor $450 to $1,100. We fix the price in writing before we start, and that is the price you pay.

Why is my LG fridge warm when the freezer is cold?

The freezer makes the cold air and a fan and damper share it with the fridge. When the freezer is cold but the fridge is not, we look for frost on the evaporator, a failed fan, a stuck damper or a faulty defrost heater or sensor.

What does Er FF mean on an LG fridge?

Er FF points to a problem with the freezer fan, and Er rF points to the fridge fan. Frost around the fan is common. LG suggests a manual defrost: unplug the fridge, empty it and leave the doors open for about 8 hours. Call us if the code returns.

My LG fridge shows Er dH. What now?

Er dH means a defrost cycle ran for more than an hour without reaching its target temperature. LG suggests unplugging the fridge for 2 minutes and letting it defrost again. If the code returns, the defrost heater, sensor or board needs testing.

Is an LG linear compressor worth repairing?

Often the compressor is not the problem. A failed inverter board, relay or fan can give the same symptoms and costs much less, so we test those first. Check your LG warranty too, because LG has offered long compressor cover on many models.

Why has my LG ice maker stopped?

Common causes are a frozen water line, a failed inlet valve, a dirty filter, an ice maker fan fault (Er IF) or a freezer that is not cold enough. Check the ice bin is seated and the door seals shut. If ice is still missing, we test the valve and fan.

Are you an LG authorised service centre?

No. We are an independent repairer and are not affiliated with LG. If your fridge is still under warranty, contact LG or your retailer first. If cover has ended, or you want a faster visit, we are happy to help.

Do you repair LG InstaView and Door-in-Door fridges?

Yes. We repair InstaView, Door-in-Door, French door, side-by-side, top mount and bottom mount LG fridges, including Smart Inverter and ThinQ models. Have your model number ready when you book. It is on a label inside the fridge.
