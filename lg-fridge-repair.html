The quick answer

LG Fridge Repairs in Parramatta, Explained

Most LG fridge faults come down to a handful of parts: a fan, a defrost heater or sensor, a door seal, a control board or, less often, the compressor. Most LG repairs here cost $150 to $450 including parts. We work on all LG fridge models, from older top mounts to InstaView, Door-in-Door, French door and side-by-side fridges.

One thing to know first: we are an independent repairer, not an LG service centre. If your fridge is still under warranty, contact LG or your retailer first. If it isn't, or you can't wait, we're ready.

What we fix

Common LG Fridge Problems We Repair

These are the faults we see most on LG fridges and freezers. If yours isn't listed, book anyway. A technician can usually find it quickly.

  • LG fridge not cooling

    The fridge is warm but the compressor runs. Often a dirty condenser, a failed fan or a sealed-system fault. See our fridge not cooling repair page.

  • Freezer cold, fridge warm

    Cold air isn't reaching the fresh-food section. Think frosted evaporator, fan, damper or defrost parts.

  • Error codes on the display

    Er FF, Er rF, Er dH, Er CF and more. The code points us to the part to test.

  • Ice and frost build-up

    Frost on the back wall or ice around a fan. Usually the defrost heater, sensor or drain.

  • Ice maker or dispenser faults

    No ice, small ice or a frozen chute. We test the water line, inlet valve, ice maker fan and sensor.

  • Water leaking inside or out

    Puddles under the fridge or water in the crisper are most often a frozen or blocked defrost drain.

  • Buzzing, clicking or loud noise

    Fan motors, a tired relay or a struggling compressor. Worth fixing before it fails.

  • InstaView and Door-in-Door faults

    Door seals, hinges, panel and display problems alongside the usual cooling parts.

  • Won't start or trips the power

    A fridge that is dead or trips the switch can have a board, relay or heater fault. Switch it off and call us.

Error codes

LG Fridge Error Codes and What to Try

A code is a clue, not a diagnosis. Here are the ones we get asked about most, based on LG's own support information. LG can change what a code means between model years, so check your manual too.

CodeUsually meansWhat you can try
Er FFFreezer fan problemOften frost around the fan. Manual defrost: unplug, empty and leave the doors open about 8 hours. If it returns, book a repair.
Er rFFridge fan problemSame cause and same defrost steps as Er FF. If it persists, book a repair.
Er dHDefrost ran over an hourUnplug for 2 minutes and let it defrost again. If it returns, the heater, sensor or board needs testing.
Er dSDefrost sensor faultThe sensor is shorted or disconnected. No DIY fix. Needs a technician.
Er IFIce maker fan problemOften frost or ice around the fan. Check the door seal and level, then defrost. If it returns, call us.
Er CFCondenser fan problemNo DIY fix. A technician tests the fan and its feedback signal.

Er 22Compressor relay problemNo DIY fix. Test the relay before blaming the compressor.

Before you reset anything: write the code down. Repeated resets can hide a fault from the technician. If a fan or defrost code keeps coming back, it needs a repair, not another reset.

Why it happens

LG Fridge Not Cooling? Start Here

An LG fridge makes its cold in the freezer and moves it to the fridge with a fan. That is why the freezer can be fine while the fridge is warm. When frost builds up on the evaporator, it blocks that airflow. The cause is usually a defrost heater, a defrost sensor or a blocked drain.

Before you call, try these quick checks: make sure the doors close fully, pull the fridge a few centimetres from the wall, and check that vents inside aren't blocked by food. If the fridge is still warm after that, book a repair. For a longer list of causes, see our guide to why a fridge stops cooling.

Warm food? Act quickly

Food that has been above 5°C for more than 4 hours should be thrown out. Keep the doors shut and move meat and dairy into a cooler with ice. If you need us after hours, see emergency fridge repair.

Linear compressor

LG Linear Compressor and Inverter Faults

Many LG fridges use an inverter linear compressor, and LG has offered long compressor warranties on many models. Before you pay for a compressor repair, check your purchase date, the model number and LG's current warranty terms. If cover is still on, it may be cheaper to claim than to pay us.

If you are out of cover, we'll test the compressor, the inverter board, the relay and the fans first. A failed board or relay is often mistaken for a dead compressor and costs far less to fix. If the sealed system is leaking, the leak is fixed first and then the gas is recharged. Our fridge regas and gas leak repair page explains how.

Honest advice

Is It Worth Repairing an LG Fridge?

Usually, yes. A good rule is to repair when the fix costs less than half the price of a comparable new fridge and the fridge is under about 10 years old. A new fan, heater, sensor or seal on a fridge in good shape is almost always worth it. A leaking sealed system on a 15-year-old fridge often is not, and we'll tell you so before you spend anything.

LG Fridge Repair Parramatta FAQs

Where can I get LG fridge repair in Parramatta?

Right here. Our licensed technicians are based locally and repair LG fridges across Parramatta and nearby suburbs. We are an independent repairer, not an LG service centre. Book online and we confirm a time, usually the same day if you book early.

How much does LG fridge repair cost in Parramatta?

Most LG fridge repairs cost $150 to $450 including parts. A call-out is usually $60 to $200. Control boards cost $250 to $600 and compressors $450 to $1,100. You get a fixed quote before we start, and that price is the one you pay.

Why is my LG fridge not cooling but the freezer is cold?

Cold air is made in the freezer and shared with the fridge by a fan and a damper. If the freezer is cold and the fridge is warm, the usual causes are frost on the evaporator, a failed fan, a stuck damper or a faulty defrost heater or sensor.

What does the Er FF error mean on an LG fridge?

Er FF points to a freezer fan problem, and Er rF points to the fridge fan. Frost around the fan is a common reason. LG suggests a manual defrost: unplug the fridge, empty it and leave the doors open for about 8 hours. If the code returns, book a repair.

My LG fridge shows Er dH. What should I do?

Er dH means a defrost cycle ran for over an hour without reaching its target temperature. LG suggests unplugging the fridge for 2 minutes and letting it defrost again. If the code comes back, the defrost heater, sensor or board needs testing.

Is it worth repairing an LG linear compressor?

Often it is not the compressor at all. A failed inverter board, relay or fan can look the same and costs far less, so we test those first. Also check your LG warranty, because LG has offered long compressor cover on many models. Cover may save you the repair bill.

Why is my LG ice maker not working?

Common causes are a frozen water line, a failed inlet valve, a dirty filter, an ice maker fan fault (Er IF) or a freezer that isn't cold enough. Check that the ice bin sits properly and the door seals shut. If ice still isn't coming, we can test the valve and fan.

Are you an LG authorised service centre?

No. We are an independent repairer and we are not affiliated with LG. If your fridge is still under warranty, contact LG or your retailer first. If the warranty has ended or you want a faster visit, we are happy to help.

Do you repair LG InstaView and Door-in-Door fridges?

Yes. We repair InstaView, Door-in-Door, French door, side-by-side, top mount and bottom mount LG fridges, including Smart Inverter and ThinQ models. Have your model number ready when you book. It is on a label inside the fridge.
