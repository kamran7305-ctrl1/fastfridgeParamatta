Cost By Repair Type

Fridge Repair Costs Explained, Repair by Repair

Here's what drives the price of each common repair, so you can make sense of a quote, ours or anyone else's.

$250 – $600

Fridge Regas Cost (Gas Refill Cost)

A fridge regas cost includes finding and repairing the leak, pulling a vacuum on the system and refilling it with the correct refrigerant (usually R600a or R134a). A fridge gas refill cost quoted without a leak repair is a warning sign: refrigerant doesn't get "used up", so if it's low there's a leak, and topping it up alone means it will fail again. All work on the gas system must be done by an ARCtick-licensed technician.

Signs: fridge slowly getting warmer over weeks, oily residue on pipes, a hissing sound.

$450 – $1,100

Fridge Compressor Replacement Cost

Compressor replacement is the most expensive fridge repair because the part is costly and the job involves cutting into the sealed system, brazing, evacuating and regassing. Inverter compressors in Samsung, LG and Panasonic fridges sit at the top of the range. Before you pay for a new compressor, make sure it's been properly tested: a failed start relay or capacitor (around $120–$250) produces almost identical symptoms.

Signs: compressor hot but not running, clicking every few minutes, fridge tripping the power.

See our compressor repair and replacement service →
$120 – $300

Fridge Seal Replacement Cost

Fridge seal replacement cost depends on whether a genuine seal is available for your model, or whether a custom seal needs to be made. Genuine seals for premium brands (Fisher & Paykel, Liebherr, Miele, Sub-Zero) cost more. A worn seal makes the compressor work harder, so replacing it often pays for itself in lower power bills.

Signs: paper slips out easily when the door is closed on it, condensation or frost near the door.

See our fridge door seal replacement service →
$150 – $300

Fridge Thermostat Replacement Cost

A typical fridge thermostat replacement cost in Sydney is $150–$300. Older fridges use a mechanical thermostat, which is quick and inexpensive to replace. Newer frost-free models use electronic temperature sensors (thermistors) linked to a control board. The sensor is cheap, but diagnosis takes longer. If the control board itself has failed, expect $250–$600.

Signs: fridge too cold or freezing food, fridge warm despite the correct setting, erratic temperatures.

See our thermostat and control board repair service (page coming soon).

$180 – $400

Fan Motor Replacement

Frost-free fridges rely on an evaporator fan to push cold air from the freezer into the fridge. When it fails, the freezer stays cold but the fridge warms up. Condenser fans (under the fridge) keep the compressor cool.

Signs: fridge warm but freezer cold, buzzing or rattling from the back.

$150 – $350

Defrost System Repair

A failed defrost heater, timer or sensor lets ice build up over the evaporator coil until it blocks airflow completely. The repair includes defrosting the coil and replacing the faulty part.

Signs: thick ice on the freezer's back wall, fridge gradually warming.

Cost By Brand

Does the Brand Change the Repair Price?

Yes, mainly because of part prices and how complex the fridge is. Two fridges with the same fault can have quite different repair costs.

Samsung Fridge Repair Cost

Most Samsung fridge repair cost falls between $180 and $500. Common jobs are iced-up evaporators (error codes such as 22E and 33E), ice maker faults and fan motors. Samsung's digital inverter compressors and inverter boards are pricier: board repairs usually run $300–$600, and compressor replacements sit at the top of the compressor range.

LG Fridge Repair Cost

Typical LG fridge repair cost is $180 to $500. LG's linear and inverter compressors are efficient but, if they fail, a replacement is one of the pricier domestic repairs. Many LG faults, such as fan errors (ER FF), ice maker issues and sensors, are far cheaper to fix.

Premium, Integrated & Budget Brands

Fisher & Paykel ActiveSmart, Liebherr, Miele, Sub-Zero and integrated fridges usually cost 15–30% more to repair because parts are dearer and removing a unit from cabinetry takes longer. But replacing one also costs thousands, so repair is usually the better value.

Budget brands (Kogan, CHiQ, Teco, Beko and similar): parts are cheaper, but if a major sealed-system fault occurs on an older budget fridge, replacement is often the smarter choice. We'll tell you honestly.

Price Factors

What Affects Fridge Repair Cost in Sydney?

Two quotes for "a fridge not cooling" can be hundreds of dollars apart. These are the six things that decide your fridge repair cost:

The actual fault

A $20 relay and a $400 compressor can produce the same symptom. That's why proper diagnosis comes before any quote.

Brand and model

Premium and inverter models have dearer parts. Some imported brands need parts ordered in, which adds time.

Type of fridge

Integrated, French-door and side-by-side fridges take longer to access than a standard top-mount.

Sealed-system work

Anything involving the refrigerant (leaks, regas, compressors) needs licensed work, special tools and more time.

When you book

After-hours, weekend and public holiday call-outs cost more than a weekday booking.

Access and location

Tight cabinetry, stairs, or a commercial kitchen that can't shut down all add time. Parramatta and nearby suburbs keep our travel costs low.

Honest Advice

Is It Worth Paying for a Fridge Repair?

A good rule of thumb is to repair if the fridge repair cost is less than half the price of a comparable new fridge and the fridge is under about 10 years old. Most fridges in Australia last around 10–15 years. A $250 fan motor on a 6-year-old $2,000 fridge is an easy yes. A $900 compressor on a 13-year-old $900 fridge usually isn't.

Situation Our usual advice
Under ~8 years old, common part failure (seal, fan, thermostat, relay) Repair: quick, cost-effective fix
Repair cost under half of a new equivalent fridge Usually repair, especially with a 1-year warranty
Premium French-door, integrated or built-in fridge Often repair: replacement is expensive
10+ year-old budget fridge with compressor or sealed-system failure Often replace
Still under manufacturer warranty Contact the manufacturer or retailer first

Don't forget the Australian Consumer Law guarantees. If a fairly new fridge fails early, you may be entitled to a free repair or replacement from the retailer or manufacturer, even after the warranty has ended. Check before paying for a repair.

6 Ways to Keep Your Fridge Repair Cost Down

01

Book early

A fridge that's "just a bit warm" is often a cheap fan or relay fix. Leave it and a struggling compressor can fail too.

02

Book during business hours

If food is safe, a weekday booking avoids after-hours rates.

03

Have the model number ready

It's on a sticker inside the door. We can check part prices and bring the right part, avoiding a second visit.

04

Clean the condenser coils

Vacuuming the coils twice a year keeps the compressor cool and cuts running costs, especially in a Western Sydney summer.

05

Check the door seals

Close the door on a sheet of paper. If it slides out easily, the seal is leaking cold air and costing you money.

06

Ask for a phone estimate first

Describe the symptoms and model when you call. We can often give a likely price range for free, and sometimes talk you through a simple fix.
