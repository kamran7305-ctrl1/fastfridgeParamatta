Source: `claude/Keyword_Strategy_fridgerepairparramatta.xlsx` (Semrush AU, researched 16 Oct 2026, KD 0-20). Saved 16 Oct 2026 at the user's request so the user does not have to say which page to build each day. Progress lives in `claude/page-progress.md`.

## Rules from the sheet
- 32 pages in plan: 9 existing to optimise, 23 new (1 conditional). 256 keywords, avg KD 10.9.
- Volumes and KD are Australia-wide Semrush estimates, not Parramatta only. KD above 20 (e.g. "fridge repair near me" KD 54) deliberately excluded.
- Brand keywords are labelled Navigational by Semrush but searchers want a repair. Kept on purpose.
- US brands (Maytag, Amana, GE, Jenn-Air) only on the Brands hub, confirm service first.
- **URL rule:** the live site uses FLAT URLs. The sheet's planned URLs (/services/..., /commercial/..., /brands/...) are NOT used. Use the "Live URL" column below. Do not rename live pages.
- Priority order from the sheet: 1 basics (phone, NAP) 2 optimise live money pages 3 commercial cluster (COMM-FRIDGE, COOLROOM, COMM-FREEZER, ICE) 4 SEAL and FREEZER 5 brand pages (Samsung, LG, Westinghouse, Fisher & Paykel, then Electrolux, Haier, Hisense, Miele) 6 /areas hub, Blacktown, Hills District 7 blogs (leaking water first).
