Warning Signs

Signs of a Failing Fridge Compressor

None of these proves the compressor has failed. They tell us where to test.

Fridge silent and warm

The light works but nothing runs. Could be power, a relay, the board or the compressor.

Clicking on and off

A relay or overload tripping repeatedly. Often a cheap part, sometimes the compressor itself.

Compressor very hot

Running hard without cooling can mean a blocked system, low gas or a worn compressor.

Runs all the time, never cold

The compressor is working but the system is not moving heat. Test before you assume it has failed.

Trips the power

A compressor with a shorted winding can trip the safety switch. Switch off and call us.

Loud buzzing or knocking

A struggling compressor or a loose mount. Worth checking before it fails.

If the fridge is simply not cold, start with our fridge not cooling repair page.

Is It the Compressor?

Is It Really the Compressor? How We Test

Many fridges sold as having a dead compressor have a cheaper fault. This is the order we check.

01

Rule out the easy causes

Power, thermostat, condenser, fans and door seals. If one of these is the cause, no compressor work is needed.

02

Test the start parts

Start relay, overload and capacitor, which look like a dead compressor but cost far less.

03

Check the control board

On inverter fridges the board drives the compressor. A failed board gives the same symptoms.

04

Test the compressor

Electrical tests and pressure checks show whether the compressor and the sealed system are healthy.

05

Quote, then decide

You see the price and our repair-or-replace advice before any work starts.

Inverter and Linear Compressors

Inverter Compressors in Samsung, LG and Other Brands

Many newer fridges use an inverter compressor, and some brands have offered extended compressor warranties.

An inverter compressor is driven by a control board that changes its speed. A failed board gives the same symptoms as a failed compressor, and costs far less. Before you pay for a new compressor, check your purchase date, the model number and the brand's current warranty terms. If cover is still on, it may be cheaper to claim than to pay us.

What Is Involved

What a Compressor Replacement Involves

It is a sealed-system job, so it is longer and needs licensed work.

What we do

  • Recover the refrigerant and open the sealed system.
  • Braze in the new compressor and a new filter-drier.
  • Pull a deep vacuum to remove air and moisture.
  • Weigh in the exact gas charge from the rating plate.
  • Test the fridge for cooling and leaks.

What to expect

  • A fixed quote before any work starts.
  • A timeframe if the compressor has to be ordered.
  • Several hours for the fridge to settle before it reaches temperature.
  • A 1-year warranty on parts and labour.
