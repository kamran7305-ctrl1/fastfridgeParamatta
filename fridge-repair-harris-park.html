When a Fridge Fails

Fridge Failed Mid-Service? Five Steps to Protect Your Stock

Cold food should stay at 5°C or below. Food above 5°C for more than 4 hours should be thrown out.

  1. 1Stop opening doorsEach opening lets the cold out. Close the coolroom and fridge doors and leave them.
  2. 2Look at the powerPlug, breaker and isolator switch. A tripped switch is the cheapest fault to fix.
  3. 3Save the risky stock firstMeat, dairy and seafood go into a working fridge or a cooler with ice.
  4. 4Use a thermometerCheck the real temperature with a probe. Cold food should stay at 5°C or below.
  5. 5Send us the detailsBook online or email the equipment, brand and what it is doing, and we line up the visit.
