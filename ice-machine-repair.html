Why Ice Machines Fail

Why Commercial Ice Machines Break Down

Most faults come from water, heat and a lack of cleaning. Fixing the cause stops the repeat call.

Hard water and scale

Scale clogs the water lines and coats the evaporator, so the machine makes thin, cloudy ice or stops altogether.

Dirty condensers

A coil clogged with dust and grease cannot shed heat, so the machine runs hot and makes less ice.

Old filters and no cleaning

A worn filter and a dirty water path cause smell, taste problems and slow ice. Regular cleaning prevents most of it.

If the fault points to the gas system, we find the leak first and recharge after, never a top-up on its own. Our fridge regas and gas leak repair page explains how.

Before You Call

Ice Machine Not Working? Quick Checks First

Two minutes of checks can save a call-out. If none of these help, we will come out.

Try this

  • Check the water tap to the machine is fully on.
  • Check the bin is seated and the door is shut. A full or misplaced bin stops the machine.
  • Look for a tripped breaker or a switched-off isolator.
  • Make sure there is clear space around the machine so air can reach the condenser.
  • Write down any alarm light or code and the brand and model plate details.

Avoid this

  • Do not serve ice that smells, tastes off or looks dirty. Ice is food.
  • Do not chip ice out of the machine with a tool. You can puncture the evaporator.
  • Do not add refrigerant yourself. It needs an ARC licence.
  • If you smell burning or see water near the electrics, switch it off at the wall and call us.

Ice Machine Service

Commercial Ice Machine Servicing and Cleaning

Regular cleaning keeps ice tasting right and stops most breakdowns.

What a service visit covers

  • Descaling the water system
  • Cleaning and sanitising the machine and bin
  • Water filter check and replacement
  • Condenser coil and fan cleaning
  • Valve, sensor and drain checks

Easy habits between visits

  • Use a clean scoop and keep it out of the bin.
  • Never store food or drinks in the ice bin.
  • Keep a gap around the machine for airflow.
  • Watch for slow ice or odd taste and call us early.
