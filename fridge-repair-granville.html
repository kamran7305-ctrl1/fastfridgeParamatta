Equipment Down

Business Fridge or Coolroom Stopped? Protect Your Stock First

Cold food should stay at 5°C or below. These steps help while you wait for us.

In the first hour

  • Keep doors shut and do not prop a coolroom door open.
  • Check the power, isolator and any tripped breaker.
  • Move meat, dairy and seafood to a working fridge or a cooler with ice.
  • Read the temperature on a probe thermometer.

When you book

  • Give the street address, unit and any gate code.
  • Tell us about loading dock times and site access rules.
  • Name the equipment, the brand and what it is doing.
  • Say how urgent it is so we plan the visit.

Food above 5°C for more than 4 hours should be thrown out. Need us out of hours? See emergency fridge repair.
