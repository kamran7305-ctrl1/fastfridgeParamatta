Why Freezers Fail

Why Commercial Freezers Break Down

Freezers run colder and harder than fridges, so a few causes show up again and again.

Doors opened all day

Every opening lets in warm, damp air that turns to frost, so the freezer works harder and the seals wear faster.

Hot, greasy kitchens

Dust and grease clog the condenser and the unit cannot shed heat, so it overheats and runs flat out.

Failed defrost

If the defrost heater, timer or sensor fails, ice builds on the coil until air cannot move and the freezer warms.

If the fault points to the gas system, we find the leak first and recharge after, never a top-up on its own. Our fridge regas and gas leak repair page explains how.

When It Stops

Commercial Freezer Stopped? What to Do First

Frozen stock holds its cold for a while if you do the right things. Here is how to buy time.

Do this

  • Keep the door or lid shut. A full, closed freezer stays cold far longer than one opened repeatedly.
  • Check the power: the plug, the breaker and any isolator switch.
  • If you have a second working freezer, move the most valuable or high-risk stock first.
  • Check the food: ice crystals and hard food are a good sign.
  • Write down the brand, model and any error code, and tell us when you book.

Avoid this

  • Do not chip ice off the coil with a tool. A punctured coil is a costly repair.
  • Do not refreeze food that has thawed and sat above 5°C for more than 4 hours.
  • Do not add refrigerant yourself. It needs an ARC licence.
  • If you smell burning or hear sparking, switch it off at the wall and call us.

Freezer Maintenance

Commercial Freezer Service to Prevent Breakdowns

A short visit before the busy season costs far less than a failed freezer and a skip full of stock.

What a service visit covers

  • Condenser coil and fan cleaning
  • Door and lid seal checks
  • Defrost heater, timer and sensor checks
  • Drain and drip tray checks
  • Temperature and controller checks

Easy checks between visits

  • Leave a gap around the unit so air can move.
  • Do not pack stock against the fan or air vents.
  • Wipe seals and check the door closes fully.
  • Look for frost building around the door or lid.
