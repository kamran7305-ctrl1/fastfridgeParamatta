Brands Others Turn Away

Teco, TCL, Vintec, Kogan, GE, Simpson & Siemens Fridge Repairs

Many repairers only want the big names. We repair these brands every month across Parramatta, and below is what we typically find with each.

Teco Fridge Repair Parramatta

TECO is a Taiwanese brand sold in Australia mainly as good-value top-mount fridges, bar fridges, upright freezers and chest freezers. You'll find plenty of them in Parramatta apartments, granny flats and garages, often as a second fridge.

Our Teco fridge repairs focus on making sure the repair is worth it: these are affordable fridges, so we diagnose first and give you an honest repair-or-replace recommendation before any work starts.

Common faults we fix:

Thermostat failure: fridge too warm or freezing food

Start relay or capacitor faults: clicking, compressor won't start

Worn door seals causing condensation and frost

Ice build-up in manual-defrost freezers and bar fridges

Still under warranty? Contact TECO Australia first. Out of warranty, we can usually fix it the same day.

Book a Teco Repair

TCL Fridge Repair Parramatta

TCL has grown quickly in Australia, from frost-free top and bottom mounts to French-door and side-by-side fridges with inverter compressors and electronic controls. Many TCL fridges in Parramatta are only a few years old.

TCL fridge repairs usually involve the electronics or airflow rather than the compressor. We read the fault symptoms, test the sensors and fans, and replace only what has actually failed.

Common faults we fix:

Temperature sensor or control board errors

Evaporator fan faults: fridge warm, freezer cold

Defrost faults causing an iced-up back wall

Door alignment and seal problems on multi-door models

A newer TCL may still be under manufacturer warranty. Check that first, as warranty repairs must go through TCL's service network.

Book a TCL Repair

Vintec Fridge Repair Parramatta

Vintec is a specialist wine-cabinet brand (part of the Electrolux group), with single-zone, dual-zone and multi-zone wine fridges designed to hold a stable temperature and humidity. A small drift can affect a valuable collection, so Vintec problems deserve a technician who understands wine storage, not just fridges.

Our Vintec fridge repairs and wine fridge repairs cover compressor-based cabinets: zone temperature drift, fans, controllers, door seals and condensation issues. We check both zones and the cabinet's ventilation, which is often the hidden cause in built-in installations.

Common faults we fix:

One zone warmer than its setting, or zones drifting

Fan or controller faults, a blank or flashing display

Excess condensation or low humidity

Door seal and hinge wear on glass doors

Under warranty, book through Vintec's own service (1800 666 778). Out of warranty, we can help, and we'll confirm parts availability before quoting.

Book a Vintec Repair

Kogan Fridge Repair Parramatta

Kogan sells its own-label fridges, freezers and bar fridges online. They're made by contract manufacturers, so the parts inside are often standard components, but brand-specific parts can be harder to find than for mainstream brands.

For Kogan fridge repairs we diagnose the fault, then source genuine or quality-matched compatible parts. If a part simply isn't available, we'll tell you upfront so you don't pay for a repair that can't be finished.

Common faults we fix:

Not cooling: relay, thermostat or fan faults

Frost build-up and defrost faults

Noisy fans and compressors

Cracked door shelves and worn seals

Recently bought? Check your Kogan warranty and your Australian Consumer Law rights before paying for a repair.

Book a Kogan Repair

GE Fridge Repair Parramatta

GE (General Electric) fridges, now under GE Appliances, a Haier company, are less common in Australia. Most we see in Parramatta are larger imported side-by-side and French-door models, often with plumbed ice and water dispensers.

GE fridge repairs often involve the ice and water system or the defrost and control electronics. Some GE parts have to be imported, so we confirm availability and timing before you commit.

Common faults we fix:

Ice maker not making ice; water valve faults

Dispenser leaks and frozen water lines

Defrost heater, sensor or timer failure

Control board and display faults

Older GE models: we'll tell you honestly if part availability makes replacement the better option.

Simpson Fridge Repair Parramatta

Simpson is a long-standing Australian appliance brand, now owned by Electrolux. Older Simpson top-mount fridges are still running in many established Parramatta, North Parramatta and Northmead homes, and they're often worth keeping.

Because Simpson shares many parts with Westinghouse and Electrolux, Simpson fridge repairs are usually straightforward and parts are easy to get. Many are fixed on the first visit from parts carried in the van.

Thermostat failure

Siemens Fridge Repair Parramatta

Siemens fridges are German-engineered by BSH, the same group that makes Bosch. Many Siemens models in Parramatta are integrated (built-in) fridges or premium NoFrost freestanding units with electronic controls.

Siemens fridge repairs and integrated fridge repairs need care: we work inside the cabinetry without damaging joinery, and test the electronic module, sensors and NoFrost system before quoting.

NoFrost defrost faults: ice on the rear panel

Electronic module or sensor errors

Integrated door hinge and seal problems

Under warranty, use Siemens' own repair service. Out of warranty, our Siemens repairs are backed by our 1-year warranty.

Honest Advice

Manufacturer Service or an Independent Repairer?

Call the manufacturer when…

  • Your fridge is still under the manufacturer's warranty. Using them keeps that cover intact.
  • It's a recent purchase with an early fault. Your Australian Consumer Law rights may give you a free repair or replacement through the retailer.

Call us when…

  • The fridge is out of warranty and you want a fast, local fix.
  • You have more than one brand to look at, for example a fridge and a bar fridge.
  • The manufacturer's wait time is too long for a fridge full of food.
  • You want an honest second opinion on whether a repair is worth it.

Genuine fridge parts vs quality-matched parts

We use genuine fridge parts wherever they're available. For brands with limited part supply, such as some Kogan and GE models, we may recommend a quality-matched compatible part. We'll always tell you which you're getting, and both are covered by our 1-year warranty.
