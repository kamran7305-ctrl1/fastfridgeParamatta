Common Fridge Problems We See in Parramatta

What you notice, what usually causes it, what we check and a typical price.

What you seeLikely causeWhat we checkTypical cost
Fridge warm, freezer cold Iced evaporator, failed fan or defrost fault Check the fan, defrost heater and sensor $150 to $450
Fridge and freezer both warm, compressor running Dirty condenser, failed fan or low gas Clean the coil, test the fan and the sealed system $150 to $450
Fridge silent and warm Start relay, board or compressor fault Test the relay and board first, then the compressor $120 to $1,100
Door will not seal, frost near the door Worn door seal or hinge Replace the seal and check alignment $120 to $300
Water under the fridge Blocked or frozen defrost drain Clear the drain and check the defrost parts $150 to $350
Loud buzzing or rattling Failing fan motor or ice on a fan blade Defrost, replace the fan if needed $180 to $400
