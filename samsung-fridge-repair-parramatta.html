Samsung Fridge Repair in Parramatta: What to Expect

Samsung fridges usually fail in one of a few places: a fan, the defrost heater or sensor, a door seal, the control board and, less often, the compressor. Typical repairs here cost $150 to $450 with parts. We repair every layout, from older top mounts to French door, side-by-side and Family Hub models.

We are an independent business and not a Samsung service centre. While your fridge is under warranty, start with Samsung or the retailer. Once cover has ended, or you need a faster visit, call on us.

What we fix

Samsung Fridge Problems We Repair Most

Here are the faults that bring Samsung owners to us. If yours is not on the list, book anyway, because a technician usually finds the cause quickly.

  • Warm fridge, running compressor

    The compressor hums along but the fridge stays warm. A dirty condenser, a dead fan or a sealed-system problem are the usual suspects. Our fridge not cooling repair page has the wider picture.

  • Cold freezer, warm fridge

    Cold air never reaches the fresh-food side. We look at the evaporator, fan, damper and defrost parts.

  • Frost or a block of ice

    Frost across the back panel or a sheet of ice in the freezer normally comes from the defrost heater, sensor or drain.

  • Ice maker and dispenser faults

    No ice, tiny ice or a frozen chute. We test the water line, inlet valve and ice maker module. See the ice maker section below.

  • Leaks inside and outside

    Water on the floor or in the crisper drawers is usually a blocked or frozen defrost drain.

  • Noise: clicking, buzzing, scraping

    Fan motors, a worn start relay or ice catching a fan blade. Cheaper to fix early than after it fails.

  • Codes and dead displays

    21E, 22E, 88 88, a blank panel or a fridge stuck in demo mode.

  • Power trips

    A fridge that knocks out the switch can have a heater or compressor fault. Unplug it and call us.

  • Family Hub and Bespoke issues

    Screen, Wi-Fi and panel faults sit on top of the usual cooling parts. We check the cooling side first.

Error codes

Samsung Fridge Error Codes and What to Try

A code points us to a system, it does not name the broken part. Samsung can change what a code means between model years, so check your manual too.

CodeUsually meansWhat you can try
21E / 21CFreezer fanLook for ice around the fan and check the door seals. Defrost the freezer and see if it clears. If it returns, book a repair.
22E / 22CFridge fanOften a fan fault or a door not closing. Leave the fridge unplugged with the doors open for a few hours. If the code comes back, call us.
5E / 5CDefrost sensorThe sensor cannot read the evaporator temperature. A technician tests and replaces it.
88 88Power or communication errorSwitch off at the wall for 1 minute and restart. That usually clears it.
OFF OFF / OF OFCooling Off (demo) modeNot a fault. Hold the top two left-hand buttons for 5 to 8 seconds until it chimes.
Ice Off blinkingIce bucket not seatedPull the bucket out and push it back until it clicks.

Write the code down first. Resetting again and again can hide the fault from the technician. A fan or defrost code that keeps returning needs a repair, not another reset.

Ice and water

Samsung Ice Maker and Water Dispenser Not Working

Ice makers are one of the most common Samsung call-outs. Work through this list before you book.

CheckWhat it tells us
Is the ice bucket seated and the door shut?An unseated bucket stops the machine and makes Ice Off blink.
Is the freezer cold enough?Ice only forms if the freezer holds its temperature. A warm freezer is the root fault.
Is the water line frozen or kinked?A frozen line gives small ice or none. A kink behind the fridge can do the same.
Is the filter old?A clogged filter slows water flow and affects both ice and dispensed water.
Does water still reach the dispenser?If the dispenser works but ice does not, the ice maker module is more likely at fault.

If those checks do not fix it, we test the water inlet valve and the ice maker module. See our fridge repair cost guide for typical prices.

Why it happens

Samsung Fridge Not Cooling? Start Here

In most Samsung fridges the freezer produces the cold and a fan carries part of it into the fridge. That is why a warm fridge and a cold freezer can sit side by side. Ice on the evaporator blocks the airflow, and the cause is normally a defrost heater, a defrost sensor or a blocked drain.

Before you call, shut every door firmly, pull the fridge a few centimetres off the wall and clear any food away from the vents. Still warm? Book a repair. Our guide to why a fridge stops cooling lists the other causes.

Food getting warm?

Anything held above 5°C for more than 4 hours should be thrown out. Keep the doors shut and put meat and dairy in a cooler with ice. After hours, use our emergency fridge repair page.

Compressor

Samsung Inverter Compressors and Warranty Cover

Samsung has offered extended compressor warranties on some models, so check before you pay. Look up your purchase date, the model number and Samsung's current terms. If you are still covered, a claim may cost less than a repair.

If cover has ended, we test the compressor, the inverter board and the start parts, because a failed board or relay often looks like a dead compressor and costs far less. Gas leaks are repaired first and then the system is recharged. Our compressor repair and fridge regas pages explain both jobs.

Samsung Fridge Repair Parramatta FAQs

Where can I get Samsung fridge repair in Parramatta?

With us. Our licensed technicians live and work locally and fix Samsung fridges across Parramatta and the suburbs around it. We are an independent repairer, not a Samsung service centre. Book online and we confirm a time, often the same day if you book early.

How much does Samsung fridge repair cost in Parramatta?

Most Samsung repairs cost $150 to $450 with parts included, and the call-out is usually $60 to $200. A control board runs $250 to $600 and a compressor $450 to $1,100. The price is fixed in writing before we begin, and that is the amount you pay.

Why is my Samsung fridge warm while the freezer is still cold?

The freezer makes the cold air, and a fan pushes some of it into the fridge through a damper. Ice on the evaporator, a failed fan, a damper stuck shut or a faulty defrost heater or sensor will starve the fridge side while the freezer looks fine.

What does the 22E code mean on a Samsung fridge?

On most models 22E (or 22C) points to the fridge fan and 21E points to the freezer fan. Ice around the fan is a common trigger. Codes can differ between models, so note the code, check your manual and book a visit if it comes back.

Is it safe to reset a Samsung fridge that shows an error?

A power reset is fine for 88 88, which Samsung treats as a power or communication fault. Switch off at the wall for about a minute, then back on. For fan and defrost codes, repeated resets can hide the fault, so write the code down and call us instead.

My Samsung ice maker stopped. What should I check?

Check that the ice bucket is pushed in until it clicks, since an unseated bucket makes Ice Off blink. After that, the usual causes are a frozen water line, a blocked or failed inlet valve, a worn filter, a faulty ice maker module or a freezer that is not cold enough.

Do you work for Samsung?

No. We are independent and not affiliated with Samsung. If your fridge is still under warranty, contact Samsung or your retailer first. If cover has ended, or you would rather not wait, we are glad to help.

Should I repair an older Samsung fridge or replace it?

If the repair costs less than half the price of a similar new fridge and the fridge is under about 10 years old, repairing usually makes sense. A leak in the sealed system on an old fridge changes the sums, and we will say so before you spend anything.

Can you repair Samsung French door, side-by-side and Family Hub models?

Yes. We repair French door, side-by-side, top mount, bottom mount and multi-door Samsung fridges, plus Bespoke and Family Hub models. Have the model number handy when you book. It sits on a label inside the fridge.
