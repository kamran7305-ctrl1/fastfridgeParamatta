Is It An Emergency?

Urgent Fridge Repair Parramatta: When to Call Us

Not every fault needs an after-hours call-out, and we'll tell you honestly if yours can wait until morning. Book an urgent fridge repair in Parramatta when any of these apply:

Your fridge is above 5°C and rising

Food Standards Australia New Zealand advises keeping chilled food at 5°C or colder. If a thermometer shows your fridge is climbing past that and not recovering, the clock is running on everything inside.

The freezer is defrosting

Soft ice cream, sweating frozen goods or water pooling under the freezer means it has lost temperature. A full, closed freezer buys you time, but not all night.

The fridge keeps tripping the power

Repeated tripping of the safety switch points to an electrical fault in the compressor, defrost heater or wiring. Unplug the fridge and stop resetting the breaker. This is a safety call-out.

Burning smell, smoke or sparking

Switch off at the wall straight away and leave the fridge unplugged. If there is any sign of fire, call 000 first.

Business stock is at risk

A cafe under-bench fridge, restaurant coolroom or shop display failing mid-trade means lost stock and lost sales. Commercial food-safety call-outs are prioritised.

A medical or pharmacy fridge alarm

Vaccines and many medicines must stay between 2°C and 8°C. Clinics, pharmacies and aged-care facilities are treated as priority jobs.

Can it wait until tomorrow?

If the fridge is still cooling but is noisy, has a small leak, a worn door seal or an ice maker that has stopped, it usually isn't an emergency. Book a standard same-day repair instead and save on after-hours rates.

While You Wait

Fridge Stopped Working? 6 Things to Do Before We Arrive

These steps protect your food and help our technician fix the fault faster. None of them need tools.

01

Keep the doors closed

A closed fridge generally keeps food cold for about 4 hours. A full freezer can hold for around 48 hours (about 24 hours if half full). Every opening lets the cold out.

02

Check the power supply

Plug another appliance into the same socket, check the switchboard, and make sure the fridge hasn't been switched off at the wall by accident.

03

Check the settings

Make sure the temperature control hasn't been bumped, and that 'holiday', 'demo' or 'showroom' mode isn't switched on. Demo mode is a common reason a fridge has lights but no cooling.

04

Move high-risk food

Shift meat, seafood, dairy and cooked leftovers into an esky with ice or into a second fridge.

05

Note the details

Write down the brand, the model number (usually on a sticker inside the door), any error code on the display, and when the problem started. It helps us bring the right part.

06

Follow the 2-hour / 4-hour rule

Food above 5°C for less than 2 hours can go back in the fridge or be used. Between 2 and 4 hours, use it straight away. Over 4 hours, throw it out. Never taste food to test it.

Please don't: Chip ice off the freezer walls with knives, heat the coils with a hair dryer, or keep resetting a tripping breaker. These can turn a simple repair into an expensive one, or create a safety risk.
